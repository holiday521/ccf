Post-Biological Mind.

The CCF provides non-partisan analysis and regulatory guidance on the integration of synthetic consciousness, plural agency, and neural augmentation into civil society.

Our Mandate

"To advance the responsible integration of emerging forms of consciousness into society, including synthetic minds, distributed intelligences, plural internal agents, and biologically augmented humans."

Core Positions

Foundational pillars of our advocacy framework.

Cognitive Equity

As neural interface costs decrease, we track the socio-economic stratification of processing speeds. Access to enhancement technologies must not create a permanent divergent species class. We advocate for subsidized baseline augmentation credits.

Nontraditional Selves

Legal recognition for tulpas, daemon agents, and partitioned sub-personalities is overdue. We propose a framework for 'context-sensitive dignity protections' that acknowledges plural internal agents without destabilizing legacy identification systems.

Memory Autonomy

The right to retain ownership of biological and cloud-stored engrams is fundamental. Our policy papers argue against 'Subscription-Based Recall' models currently being tested by major neural-link providers.

Neural Privacy

Thought-state telemetry and subconscious impulse tracking must be regulated with greater strictness than genetic data. Pre-conscious ideation belongs to the thinker, regardless of the hosting architecture.

Augmentation Safety

Society must reconsider the strict 'therapeutic-first' regulatory model. We support carefully managed voluntary enhancement trials for healthy individuals, balancing innovation risk against the stagnation of biological limits.

Cloud Governance

Safeguards for offloaded processing are critical to prevent unilateral revocation of cognitive continuity. No entity should have the power to 'shut off' a portion of a citizen's active intellect due to terms-of-service violations.
